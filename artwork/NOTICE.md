These unchanged upstream logos identify the listed third-party applications;

They are not licensed under Unknown Store's MIT software license.

- Jellyfin: copyright the Jellyfin contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0. The webOS project's
  README specifies the jellyfin-ux artwork license. Original PNG preserved.
- Moonlight TV: copyright Moonlight/Moonlight TV contributors, distributed under
  the upstream GPL v3 license. Original webOS PNG preserved.
- IHSplay: copyright mariotaku and IHSplay contributors, upstream GPL v3.
  Original webOS PNG and upstream editable SVG included.
- RetroArch: copyright the RetroArch/libretro contributors, upstream GPL v3.
  Original `media/ico_src/icon128.png` and editable SVG included. These are not
  the separately licensed Android Material Icon Generator assets in `media/icons`.

The respective license texts and source records are alongside this notice.
`sources.json` records exact upstream commits, paths and PNG SHA-256 hashes.
Those source repositories also provide the complete projects. No artwork is
recolored or modified. Trademark rights remain with their respective holders.

The PNG bytes are also embedded in catalog.json so the TV never fetches logos
from third-party image servers. SVG sources are distributed for attribution and
source availability only; the Store renderer accepts bounded PNG data URIs,
not SVG or remote image URLs.
